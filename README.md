# My Favorite Series
## Attack on Titan
**Attack on Titan is my favorite series of all time because it has such an amazing story and deep characters. It’s about humanity fighting against giant creatures called Titans that threaten their survival. I really admire Eren and his determination to protect his friends and discover the truth about their world. The show is full of action, mystery, and emotion, and it always keeps me excited and curious about what will happen next.**
